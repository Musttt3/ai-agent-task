# Prompt Gambar — "Punya Helikopter 30 Ribu Tahun Lalu"

Untuk Google Flow (agent batch). Format 9:16, 145 gambar, satu gambar per timestamp.

Cara pakai:
1. Upload gambar karakter stickman sebagai referensi (Ingredients / reference image) dan pastikan referensi itu ikut dipakai di SETIAP gambar.
2. Tempel **BAGIAN A (instruksi global)** ke agent satu kali di awal.
3. Tempel **BAGIAN B (daftar scene)** per 20–24 scene. Setiap scene sudah berisi kunci karakter sendiri, jadi tetap aman walaupun agent "lupa" Bagian A.
4. Kalau ada gambar yang masih memunculkan orang modern, generate ulang scene itu saja.

Kenapa muncul orang modern (kaos biru)? Model menganggap "helikopter modern" butuh pilot modern, lalu stickman referensi dipakai sebagai salah satu manusia purba. Karena itu tokoh utama sekarang disebut **HERO**, ditegaskan sebagai manusia purba berambut cokelat yang menerbangkan helikopter, para pemburu dibuat berbeda jelas (rambut hitam/abu, bulu abu-abu, cat wajah merah), dan setiap scene melarang orang modern.

## BAGIAN A — Instruksi global (tempel sekali)

```
You will generate one image for EACH scene in the list I send next. One image per scene, in order, keep the scene number in the file name. Aspect ratio 9:16 vertical for every image. Use the uploaded reference image for EVERY image.

HERO = the stickman in the uploaded reference image. The HERO is a prehistoric caveman AND the owner and pilot of the helicopter. Keep the design EXACTLY the same in every image: round white head with a thick black outline, simple black dot eyes with expressive eyebrows and mouth, messy spiky BROWN hair, shaggy brown fur tunic with a cream-white fur patch on the chest, thin brown rope belt, jagged fur hem, thin black stick arms and legs, rounded black mitten hands, flat black feet. Only the pose, expression and camera angle change.

STRICT RULE: there are NO modern humans in this story. Never draw a man in a T-shirt, jeans, pilot uniform or headset. Never add an extra pilot. Whenever someone flies, sits in or steps out of the helicopter, it is the HERO in the brown fur tunic.

ART STYLE: 2D hand-drawn cartoon stickman illustration, clean bold black ink outlines, flat colors with light soft shading, simple but detailed prehistoric backgrounds (grassland, forest, river, mountains, caves), earthy palette (greens, browns, sky blue), expressive comedic faces, cinematic composition, YouTube storytelling explainer style. No text, no letters, no numbers, no captions, no watermark. Thought bubbles may contain pictures only.

RECURRING ELEMENTS (keep identical in every scene):
- HELICOPTER: a small modern private helicopter, glossy white body with one navy-blue stripe, dark-blue tinted cockpit glass, black main rotor with 4 blades, small tail rotor, black landing skids. Drawn in the same cartoon ink style but with a metallic shine so it looks expensive and out of place.
- HUNTERS: other prehistoric stickmen, NOT copies of the HERO: black or grey hair (braided, bald with beard, ponytail), grey-beige fur clothes, red face-paint stripes on the cheeks, carrying long wooden spears. Never brown messy hair like the HERO.
- CHILD: one small stickman kid, about half the HERO's height, short tufty black hair, small light-tan fur tunic. Always the same child.
- MOTHER: the child's mother, stickman woman with long dark hair tied with a leather band and a long brown fur dress.
- DASHBOARD: simple cartoon helicopter dashboard with a round fuel gauge (green zone = full, red zone = empty) and a round red warning light. No readable text on it.
```

**STYLE SUFFIX** (untuk prompt yang dibuat satuan; scene di Bagian B sudah memuatnya):

```
HERO = the exact stickman from the reference image: messy spiky BROWN hair, brown fur tunic with cream chest patch, black stick limbs; a caveman who flies the helicopter. NO modern people: no man in a T-shirt, jeans or headset. 2D cartoon stickman, bold black outlines, flat earthy colors, prehistoric setting, no text, 9:16 vertical.
```
