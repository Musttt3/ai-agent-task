# Kalau Besok Indonesia Jadi Negara Emas — Treatment

Style: Art Deco (Lemo-Opuscar `art-deco`, STYLE.md kept whole). Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~66 s.
Voice: ElevenLabs "Must4" (the user's cloned Indonesian voice), off-screen narrator; the script is the user's, read as written.
Protagonist: the user's character (curly black hair, round eyes, hoodie with a chest mark, white sneakers), redrawn as a deco figure (streamlined, ~6–7 heads, faceted lacquer planes, airbrush + gold rim). **Red hoodie** = the film's one saturated accent (user's choice).
Subtitles: Indonesian, burned in (deco subtitle card) + `.srt`. Score and foley made in code. No storyboard stop.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The exhibition.** A world's-fair of "Indonesia Emas": each claim of the script is a pavilion behind a pair of doors; the boy walks door to door. Clear and very deco, but it turns a personal morning into a tour, and the script says *you wake up, you go out, you open your phone*: a tour fights the words.
2. **The countdown dial.** One giant gauge "menuju negara maju" whose notches light per claim, intercut with small vignettes. Strong graphic spine, but the script's question at the end (*kapan?*) would be answered by the dial before it is asked.
3. **One morning, then the city.** Follow the boy through the morning the script describes (bed → TV → street → phone → school/hospital → village), every scene change a deco door split; at "Tapi tunggu dulu" the film stops dead, punctures the "everyone is rich" fantasy with a bulb sign that shorts out, then pulls back from the boy to the whole city and lets his red spread to everyone.

**Chosen: 3.** It follows the script's own second-person journey, keeps one subject (the boy = "kamu"), and has a clear turn (the freeze) and a meaning-carrying peak (the one colour becoming everyone's). Opening and ending are clocks: an alarm clock wakes him, a year dial asks *kapan?* — the bookend is the question of time.

## 2. Logline and arc

A boy wakes to the news that Indonesia has become a developed country, walks into that golden morning, and learns that "emas" means not riches for a few but a better life felt by everyone — and then the film asks him, and us, when.

- **Setup** (0–38 s): the dream morning, escalating in threes (TV → street → phone; school → hospital → village). Fast foxtrot, gold everywhere, the boy the only red thing.
- **Turn** (38–45 s): "Tapi tunggu dulu…" — music cuts, one clock tick, the doors half-close; the MILIARDER marquee lights and shorts out ("Tentu nggak!").
- **Ending** (45–66 s): opportunity, not fortune (doors and stairs for everyone); towers and tech are not the point — the red spreads from the boy to every window of the city; a year dial spins and lands on "?".

## 3. Benchmark

- **1939–40 World's Fair "city of tomorrow" promotional films** (e.g. GM's *To New Horizons*). *Learn*: optimistic-future framing, slow scale reveals from a person to a whole city, streamlined vehicles as the sign of progress. *Don't take*: any design, model city, shot or music.
- **Short "what if" explainers made for phones.** *Learn*: hook inside 3 s, one idea per screen, text that the eye finds instantly in a vertical frame, pace that alternates rush and breath. *Don't take*: flat-vector look, characters, layouts, sound logos.

## 4. Look

- Ground warm lacquer black `#120d08` → deep emerald `#0b2a24` near horizons; gold banded metal; ivory `#f3e6c8` for type, faces' highlights, light.
- Triad: **accent crimson `#c8102e`** (the boy's hoodie, later everyone) / **jewel emerald `#1f6b5a`** (secondary surfaces: tram, school roofs, phone glass) / **gold**.
- Characters deco: the boy (hero, red hoodie, curly hair as a stack of gold-rimmed scallops, almond-round eyes, one-stroke nose, white sneakers); townspeople as simple streamline silhouettes in lacquer + gold rim (no red until the peak).
- Every composition on the centre axis; type Limelight (titles), Poiret One (numerals/plaques), Josefin Sans (subtitles). Chest mark of the hoodie kept as a small ivory four-point sparkle (a deco motif that matches the original mark).
- TikTok safe area: nothing important in the bottom 330 px or the right 130 px; subtitle card centred at y ≈ 1340–1420.

## 7. Cue map

- **A: Foxtrot, 128 bpm, F major** (shots 1–7): stride piano, walking pizz bass, brushes, clarinet melody, muted-trumpet answers. Section starts and scene changes on downbeats; each counted light (bulbs, badges, house lights) on an eighth or a beat; the tram on a trumpet fall.
- **B: Stop** (shot 8): music cuts on the downbeat of "Tapi"; only a clock tick and room tone.
- **C: Comic fill** (shot 9): pizzicato question figure, xylophone run up as the marquee chases, a trombone "wah" on the short-out.
- **D: Slow ballad, 76 bpm** (shots 10–12): strings + piano, the clarinet theme from A stated whole for the first time (payoff of the fragments), swell to a fanfare chord as the red fills the city.
- **E: Unresolved ending** (shot 13): tubular bell per spin of the dial, a suspended chord that does not resolve, one tick at the end.
Voice lines placed so their starts sit on beats; music ducked ~9 dB under voice.
