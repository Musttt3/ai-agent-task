## Naskah Lengkap (tanpa timestamp)

You're a doctor in 1348, and half your town is dying. The worst part? You have absolutely no idea why. Patients arrive with high fevers, swollen lumps, and dark patches on their skin.

You try everything—herbal medicines, bloodletting, even burning aromatic herbs to purify the air. Nothing seems to work. People believe the disease comes from poisonous air or divine punishment.

But you don't know the real culprit—a bacterium called *Yersinia pestis*, often spread by infected fleas. Every morning, more bodies appear in the streets. Families abandon their homes.

Even fellow doctors begin disappearing. And now, you're developing a fever. You look down. A painful swelling has appeared beneath your arm.

Suddenly, you're no longer the doctor. You're the patient.

The Black Death would kill tens of millions across Europe. And the terrifying truth? You're fighting an invisible enemy that humanity won't scientifically identify for another 500 years.
