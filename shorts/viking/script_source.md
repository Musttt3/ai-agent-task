# You're a Viking Who Reached America Before Columbus

You're a Viking, and you've just reached America almost 500 years before Christopher Columbus.

But getting here nearly killed you.

It's around the year 1000, and you live in freezing Greenland.

When sailors tell you about a mysterious land filled with forests, you join their expedition.

Big mistake.

You spend days crossing the Atlantic in a wooden ship, battling freezing waves and terrifying storms.

Then suddenly, someone shouts, **"LAND!"**

You step onto an unfamiliar coastline.

There are enormous forests, fresh water, and valuable timber.

You help build shelters and gather supplies.

But there's one problem.

You're not alone.

Indigenous peoples have lived here for thousands of years.

And encounters between your crew and local communities could turn dangerous.

With limited supplies and no reinforcements, you eventually sail back toward Greenland.

A thousand years later, archaeologists find a Norse settlement in Newfoundland, Canada.

Evidence confirms Norse activity there in 1021.

You weren't the first person in America.

But you were part of an extraordinary voyage that crossed the Atlantic centuries before Columbus.

And the craziest part?

You had absolutely no idea you were making history.
