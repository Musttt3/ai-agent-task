# Prompt Gambar — "Manusia Purba Jadi WNI"

Untuk Google Flow (agent batch). Format 16:9 (horizontal), 169 gambar, satu gambar per timestamp, dibagi 8 batch (24 scene per batch, batch terakhir 1 scene).

Cara pakai:
1. Upload gambar stickman sebagai referensi dan pastikan referensi itu dipakai di SETIAP gambar.
2. Tempel **BAGIAN A** ke agent satu kali di awal.
3. Tempel **BAGIAN B** satu batch per kali. Setiap scene sudah membawa ciri karakternya sendiri, jadi tetap aman walau agent lupa Bagian A.
4. Kalau ada gambar yang salah, generate ulang scene itu saja.

Catatan pakaian HERO:
- **Scene 001–100 (0:00–6:14):** baju bulu seperti gambar referensi.
- **Scene 101 dst. (6:17–9:42):** kaos kuning mustard + celana pendek cokelat (sesuai naskah "mulai mengenakan kaos dan celana pendek"), rambut dan wajah tetap sama.
- **Scene kilas balik (sepia)** di bagian akhir tetap memakai baju bulu.

## BAGIAN A — Instruksi global (tempel sekali)

```
You will generate one image for EACH scene in the list I send next. One image per scene, in order, keep the scene number in the file name. Aspect ratio 16:9 horizontal (landscape) for every image, with wide cinematic compositions. Use the uploaded reference image for EVERY image.

HERO = the stickman in the uploaded reference image: round white head with a thick black outline, simple black dot eyes with expressive eyebrows and mouth, messy spiky BROWN hair, shaggy brown fur tunic with a cream-white fur patch on the chest, thin rope belt, jagged fur hem, thin black stick arms and legs, rounded black mitten hands. Only pose, expression and camera angle change. The HERO is a caveman who time-travelled to modern Jakarta, Indonesia.

OUTFIT RULE: each scene says which outfit the HERO wears. "Fur tunic" = exactly as in the reference. "Modern clothes" = plain mustard-yellow T-shirt, brown shorts and sandals, but the SAME head, SAME messy brown hair and SAME face. Never put the HERO in any other outfit.

OTHER PEOPLE: modern Indonesian stickmen in the same art style (round white heads, stick limbs) with neat black hair or hijab and everyday clothes. Nobody except the HERO has messy brown hair or fur clothes (except in sepia flashbacks of the prehistoric tribe). Never replace the HERO with another person.

RECURRING SIDE CHARACTERS (keep identical each time they appear):
- OWNER: warung food stall owner, short black hair, white apron.
- FRIEND: the HERO's coworker, short black hair, thin mustache, grey work shirt.
- FOREMAN: construction foreman, big black mustache, white hard hat, clipboard.
- LANDLORD: plump man, black hair, glasses, checkered sarong.
- CHILD: small boy, short black hair, red T-shirt.
- MOTHER: the child's mother, hijab and long dress.

ART STYLE: 2D hand-drawn cartoon stickman illustration, clean bold black ink outlines, flat colors with light soft shading, detailed backgrounds (prehistoric jungle; modern Jakarta streets, alleys, warung, construction site, small rented room, commuter train), expressive comedic faces, cinematic composition, YouTube storytelling explainer style. Flashbacks to the prehistoric past use a warm sepia tone. No brand logos, no watermark, no readable text except where a scene explicitly asks for it. Thought bubbles and comment bubbles contain pictures or emoji only.
```
